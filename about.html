Our Story

Music for life, from Kira Road.

96.6 Spirit FM Kampala is a dynamic and refreshing inspirational radio station of choice, covering 13 districts of central Uganda — including Kampala, Mukono, Kamuli, Kayunga, Jinja, Kalangala, Wakiso, Luwero, Mpigi, Nakasongola, Mityana, Mubende and Kiboga.

We broadcast from Kira Road in Kampala — a home that reflects the station's roots and character.

We offer wholesome programming targeted at 18–40 year old English and Luganda speaking listeners — broadcasting 80% in English and 20% in Luganda — giving our audience a platform to talk through daily life, for productive living.

We are your Music For Life station.
